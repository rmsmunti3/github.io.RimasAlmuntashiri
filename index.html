Smoker Status Prediction from Health Insurance Records

CCDS 322 - Applied Machine Learning | University of Jeddah (May 2025)

🎯 Project Overview & Objective

Smoking status is one of the most critical risk indicators in healthcare underwriting. When self-reported data is absent or falsified, ML models can deduce status using secondary biometric and billing data. This project benchmarked 5 algorithmic setups across 1,000,000 health insurance records.

🛠️ Data Preprocessing & Leakage Prevention

  • Handled 501,166 Missing Values: (~25% in medical and family histories). Imputed numerical attributes with Median and categorical attributes with Mode.
  • Encoding: Target binary encoded (Yes=1, No=0). Label Encoding applied to categorical demographic fields.
  • Stratified Split: 80/20 train/test split strictly preserving the 50/50 balance.
  • Feature Scaling: StandardScaler fitted exclusively on training set to prevent data leakage.

📊 Benchmark Results & Comparison

Model Accuracy Precision Recall Scalability Verdict
Logistic Regression (Baseline) 74.48% 74.75% 73.97% Fast, but misses non-linear relationships
Polynomial SVM (C=5, deg=3) 81.17% - - Highest raw accuracy; O(n²) memory scaling limits 1M data
Linear SVM (C=5) 80.77% - - Good boundary, heavy memory footprint
RBF Kernel SVM (C=10) 80.24% - - Effective non-linear mapping
Random Forest (Selected) 80.79% 80.51% 81.26% Best practical production choice for full 1M dataset

💡 Business & Clinical Takeaway

Entropy feature importance demonstrated that Insurance Charges, BMI, and Age are the top 3 dominant indicators. While Polynomial SVM reached 81.17% on a sample, Random Forest matched it while remaining scalable to millions of rows.

Detecting Fake Influencers Using Multi-Layer Social Media Analysis

CCDS-431 Social Web Analytics | University of Jeddah

🎯 The Business Problem

Selecting influencers based solely on surface metrics like follower counts creates financial waste, inflated campaign metrics, and reputational risk due to bot networks. This study constructed a multi-layer framework across 1,773 accounts (845 X, 928 Instagram).

🛠️ The 4-Layer Architecture

  • Text Layer (NLP): Sentiment distribution, spam comment rates, and WordCloud n-grams showing robotic template phrases like "normal user" and "balanced activity".
  • Action Layer: Post frequency per day. Real influencers averaged ~0.36 posts/day vs ~0.29 posts/day for fake accounts.
  • Network Layer: Follower-to-following ratio distributions showed significant overlap, proving single ratios are easily spoofed by bots.
  • Hyperlink Layer: Profile bio links and security analysis for suspicious domains.

📈 Core Empirical Finding

Individual feature correlations with fake account status were weak (spam rate: +0.04, post rate: -0.02). This empirically proved that single-feature filters fail, and multi-layer integration is required for commercial fraud auditing.

Credit Card Fraud Detection - BI & Predictive Modeling

CCDS 313 Business Intelligence | University of Jeddah

🎯 Individual Contribution & Scope

While this was a collaborative course project, my primary ownership centered on the Descriptive Analytics, ETL Pipeline (Power Query), and Interactive Power BI Dashboard Layout analyzing 284,807 European card transactions.

📊 Descriptive Discovery (Power BI)

  • The Fraud Magnitude: Only 492 out of 284,807 transactions were fraudulent (0.17%), yet caused $60,130 in direct financial losses.
  • Transaction Size Divergence: Legitimate purchases averaged $88.29, while fraudulent purchases averaged $122.21, confirming amount is a valuable predictor.
  • Temporal Spikes: Fraud attempts did not occur continuously; they emerged in sharp, automated temporal spikes indicating scripted bot attacks.

🤖 The Class Imbalance Paradox (RapidMiner)

The trained Random Forest achieved 99.63% accuracy but 0% recall, illustrating the classic data science paradox where minority classes are swallowed by overwhelming majority classes, proving why SMOTE and cost-sensitive matrices are mandatory in banking fraud.

2020 US Air Quality & Ozone Pollution Dashboard

CCDS 302 Data Visualization | University of Jeddah

🎯 Project Overview

Engineered an interactive Power BI spatial dashboard visualizing outdoor EPA monitoring data to investigate ozone concentrations and AQI severity across the United States throughout 2020.

🔍 Key Analytical Insights

  • Ozone vs. AQI Correlation: Scatter plots with linear trend lines confirmed that elevated arithmetic mean ozone levels directly mapped to higher maximum AQI readings.
  • Geographical Clustering: Bubble map visuals revealed heavy concentrations of poor air quality in metropolitan and industrial regions. San Bernardino County registered the highest average AQI in the nation.
  • Seasonal Fluctuations: Area charts confirmed ozone levels rose steadily during the first half of the year, peaking sharply during warm summer months (May–June) before declining.
  • Monitoring Skewness: Donut charts demonstrated that California (42.71%), Texas (18.3%), and Florida (14.71%) accounted for the vast majority of monitoring sensor density.
